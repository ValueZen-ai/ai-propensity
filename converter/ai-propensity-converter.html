AI Propensity · by Valuezen

Turn your ChatGPT or Claude export into an evidence file

This page works entirely on your computer. It reads your chat export, keeps only counts, and gives you a small file you can check before you choose to upload it.

  • Nothing to install, no account. It all happens inside this browser tab.
  • This page can't connect to the internet. It's locked by a security setting your browser enforces. To be sure, turn off Wi-Fi before you start.
  • Your messages stay out of it. No message text, no titles and no file names go into the file. You can search it yourself before saving.

1 Download your data export

  1. Open chatgpt.com, click your profile picture, then Settings.
  2. Go to Data controls → Export data → Export.
  3. ChatGPT emails you a download link. It can take a few minutes to a few hours, and the link expires after 24 hours. Download the .zip.
  1. Open claude.ai, click your name (bottom left), then Settings.
  2. Go to Privacy → Export data.
  3. Claude emails you a download link. Download the .zip.

Use both? Export from each one and add both in step 2. You'll get one file that covers both.

2 Add your export

Drop the export .zip here or the folder it unzipped into

On a Mac, Safari often unzips downloads by itself. If you see a folder instead of a .zip, choose the folder.

3 Check what's in the file

What the file contains

    What it never contains

    • Conversation titles
    • Names of files or images you shared
    • Your name, email or account details
